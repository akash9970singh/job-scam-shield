🔖 One-click bookmark

Works in Chrome, Brave, Edge, Firefox, Safari and Opera.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Ctrl+Shift+B (⌘+Shift+B on Mac).
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar:
    🛡️ Check for job scam
  3. On LinkedIn, WhatsApp Web, Gmail or a job site, select the message and click the bookmark. Select nothing to check the whole page.

The text is passed in the part of the link after #, which browsers never send to any server.

🧩 Browser extension

Adds right-click → Check for job scam on every site, a toolbar button, and optional warning banners on suspicious Gmail emails.

  1. Open the extensions page: chrome://extensions, brave://extensions or edge://extensions.
  2. Turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder of this project.
  4. Pin Job Scam Shield from the puzzle-piece menu.
  5. For Gmail warnings: click the extension → Settings → tick Scan emails I open in Gmail.
  1. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  2. Click Load Temporary Add-on and choose extension/manifest.json.
  3. Firefox removes it when it restarts. To keep it, sign it for free as an unlisted add-on on addons.mozilla.org.

The extension talks to this server, so keep it running.

✈️ Telegram bot

Students can paste a message, forward a whole scam conversation and send /check, or send a WhatsApp export or an offer letter PDF.

  1. On Telegram, message @BotFather → /newbot → choose a name. Copy the token (keep it secret).
  2. In the project folder, copy .env.example to .env and set TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN= your token. Never upload .env.
  3. Run the bot (keep the window open):
    .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m app.bot
  4. Open your bot in Telegram and send /start. The / menu shows the commands.
