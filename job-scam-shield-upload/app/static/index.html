Red flags to remember

💸 They ask you to pay

Registration fee, "refundable" deposit, training kit, laptop charges, verification fee. Real employers never charge candidates.

📧 Gmail or Yahoo "HR"

Big companies email from their own domain (like @infosys.com), never Gmail. Watch for lookalikes like infosys-careers.co.

👍 "Like videos, earn ₹3000/day"

Task scams pay you a little first to build trust, then ask for a "VIP task" deposit you never get back.

⏰ Pressure to act fast

"Pay within 24 hours or lose your seat" is a pressure tactic. Real offers give you time.

🪪 Aadhaar, PAN or OTP over chat

Never send identity documents or OTPs on WhatsApp. Real companies collect documents on their official portal after you join.

📱 "Let's continue on Telegram"

Moving you to Telegram or WhatsApp lets scammers avoid the job portal's checks.
